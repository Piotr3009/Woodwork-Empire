Work plan: putting people on a job

Today's row has two things that read as one: "on it: Gary" with You / Gary toggles, and a "Second man: Alone / Gary" line, so it looks as if two men must share one job and the same man can be on two. The mockup replaces both with one thing: the people on the job as chips, and one button, Assign to this job, that opens the list. A man already on another job is in the list but greyed with where he is; you cannot pick him. Taking somebody off is the little cross on his chip.

A job with nobody on it says so in red on the chip line: "Nobody on it", and the warning strip already shouts about it.
