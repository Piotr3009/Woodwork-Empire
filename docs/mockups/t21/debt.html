Debt you can see

Three places, in the game's own skins: the top bar (where the arrears are invisible today), the Drop project question (a click that today is blind and, for a big job, ends the game), and the bankruptcy card (which today never fires on arrears). Figures are Piotr's from 15 May.

The red tile sits between cash and the speed chips, only while arrears exist; it pulses. Its small line counts the months to the bailiff. The warning strip under the bar says the net position against the bank's limit (1.5 times the overdraft) in one sentence. Clicking the tile opens Accounting on the arrears.

The only two-click action in the game, by design: the second click is the red one. A small job shows the same card with green figures and no warning box, so the player learns the card, not just the danger. Reputation scales with the price: -10 up to £5,000, then -1 per £2,000 to -30.
