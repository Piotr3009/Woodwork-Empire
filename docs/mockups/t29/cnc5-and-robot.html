The five axis CNC and the spraying robot in the catalogue (CLAUDE.md T29 2.5, 2.6, 2.7)

The equipment catalogue in its own folder and classes, drawn by the game's renderCatalogue (the class cards by renderMachine) and put in the modal by syncModals, for companies built by the engine: one in the 800 m² unit with the timber kit of Turn 28 and a standard booth, the same with its robot standing, the same with no booth, and one in the 400 m² unit. The five axis CNC and the robot were pushed into the engine's own tables in the generating process only, from the figures of 2.6 and 2.7, so every price, wait, power, insurance, floor, extraction, air, dust, life, place and pace line on their cards is the renderer's own. Written by hand: the Moulding line among the five axis CNC's effects, the robot's Finishing line in place of its Output line, the pictures pointed at docs/pictures-t29/ until C1 moves them, and the two family lines. The two refusals canBuy does not have yet (Needs the 800 m² unit; The hall has its spraying robot) were put through the spec's own locked path for one render each, so the locked button and the line under it are the card's own. The first use bubble is shown on the two open folders, in today's words and in the words of 2.5.2; it stands at the foot of every tab until it is dismissed. The class descriptions are the brief's, put in the catalogue's voice; the robot's is this mockup's, from its picture.
