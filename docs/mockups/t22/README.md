# Mockups for Turn 22

Pictures made by Claude in chat on 19.09.2026 and approved by Piotr the same day, except where a
picture says otherwise. Every visual change of the brief builds from these and from nothing else.

- `bubbles-v2.png`: the mark over the head (2.5): a 14 px disc with an exclamation, 6 px over
  the head at 1x, the colour classes of Turn 21, the one line paper bubble only on hover. Shown at
  3x. [PIOTR, 19.09: "a tiny bubble with an exclamation mark, the words only when I hover"]
- `pipes-A-one-path.png`: variant A of the pipe drawing (2.7): one continuous path per run, no
  joints, no shadow under the run, galvanised greys, the drop with the hose. Shown at 2x on the
  game's 48 by 24 grid. `pipes-A-vs-B-vs-C.png` is the comparison Piotr chose from (A wins:
  least code, no seams).
- `extractor-inlet-C.png`: the three ways into the extractor's mouth; Piotr chose C: the vertical
  tight to the mouth, the elbow straight into it.
- `saw-port-B.png`: the four ways onto the saw; Piotr chose B: the rear base outlet, the drop
  vanishing behind the body.
- `ports-extractors.png`, `ports-saws.png`: the measured connection points (2.8), approved by
  Piotr on 19.09 ("all the dots are fine"). `ports-spindle-moulders.png`: Claude's measurements,
  not yet confirmed by Piotr; the edgebanders' three lines in the brief's table were measured the
  same way and have no sheet yet. Phase C's pictures are where he confirms them.

Numbers on the sheets are pixels of the 2x sprite file, the whole file including its 8 px
padding, as `PORTS` in `src/engine/ports.ts` reads them.
